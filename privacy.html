Words by Cork — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 June 2026

Words by Cork is a single-player word game. It is built to need as little of your information as possible — in fact, it needs none.

Information we collect

None. Words by Cork has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers that receive your data. Your game progress, coins, streak and settings are stored only on your device and never leave it.

Analytics & tracking

We do not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party trackers. Nothing about how you play is collected, profiled, or shared.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional “Daily reminder”, the app schedules a local notification on your device. This is handled entirely by your phone’s operating system; no data is sent anywhere. You can revoke notification permission at any time in your device settings.

Children’s privacy

Because Words by Cork collects no personal information, it is safe for players of all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
